# Content guide

## Main audiences

- Engineers interested in backend and distributed systems
- Platform and infrastructure engineers
- Potential collaborators and open-source contributors
- Future employers or consulting clients

## Content pillars

1. Backend reliability and database performance
2. Distributed-system failure modes
3. Kubernetes, AWS, and platform engineering
4. Cloud-native and AI-agent security
5. Open-source project decisions and lessons

## Publishing checklist

- Is the article based on public or independently created material?
- Does it contain a concrete lesson or reproducible example?
- Are uncertainty and limitations clear?
- Does it avoid confidential names, diagrams, data, and implementation details?
- Is the title descriptive rather than sensational?
- Are code samples safe and free of credentials?
